Part II Reading comprehension (30 points)

Read the passage and choose the best answer to each question.

自动判题
计分

In the heartland of Japan, where lush green rice fields stretch as far as the eye can see, a unique and mesmerizing form of artistry has emerged – Rice Paddy Art. This captivating blend of agriculture and creativity has become a symbol of harmony between nature and human ingenuity, enchanting both locals and tourists alike.




The roots of Rice Paddy Art can be traced back to a small village in Japan, called Inakadate. In the early 1990s, local farmers were inspired to transform their rice fields into works of art after seeing crop circles in the UK and images of an intricate rice paddy artwork in China. Eager to create something unique, they embarked on a journey to merge agriculture and art.




Creating Rice Paddy Art is no small feat; it requires meticulous planning, teamwork, and a deep understanding of agriculture. The process typically begins in the winter months when a group of artists and farmers come together to brainstorm and design the artwork.




Once the design is finalized, the farmers plant different varieties of rice, each with its own unique color, to bring the artwork to life. By carefully selecting and arranging these rice varieties, they can create detailed and vibrant images. The timing of planting is crucial, as the various rice varieties grow at different rates, allowing the artwork to develop gradually. To ensure the accuracy of their creation, the farmers use advanced technology to mark the planting locations precisely. As the rice plants grow, they require regular maintenance to retain the clarity and vibrancy of the design.




Rice has always held immense cultural significance in Japan, as it is a staple food and an integral part of the country's heritage. Rice Paddy Art not only showcases the importance of rice cultivation but also honors Japan's rich cultural traditions. Additionally, it promotes community engagement and pride among residents, who are proud to be custodians of these living canvases.




Beyond their visual appeal, these rice paddy artworks offer a unique opportunity for cultural exchange and education. Visitors, both domestic and international, gain a deeper appreciation for the meticulous work that goes into rice cultivation and the harmony between man and nature.




Rice Paddy Art has also brought positive economic benefits to the communities where it is practiced. Local businesses have profited from the influx of tourists drawn to this one-of-a-kind art form, helping to support rural areas and promote their sustainability.




Rice Paddy Art is a remarkable fusion of agriculture and artistry that has captivated people worldwide. It exemplifies the creative potential of communities united in celebrating their heritage and promoting their culture, and stands as a testament to human ingenuity and the enduring bond between people and the land they cultivate.
